Medicine Cabinet

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Medicine Cabinet ("we", "us", "our", or the "App") is a medication organization and reminder application designed to help users organize medicines, create medication reminders, track reminder activity, manage doctor appointment reminders, maintain locally stored records, and access general medicine reference information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Medicine Cabinet.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of the App.

Medicine Cabinet is designed as an organization and reminder tool. It does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medicines, provide personalized treatment recommendations, or replace a qualified healthcare professional.

2. Health and Medical Disclaimer

Important: Medicine Cabinet is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

Medicine Cabinet is intended to help users organize medication schedules, receive reminders, record reminder activity, manage appointment reminders, and access general educational medicine information.

The App does not provide personalized medical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or automatic dosage recommendations.

Any dose or quantity entered into the App is information supplied by the user and is intended only for organizing the user's medication reminder schedule. The App does not determine or calculate a personalized dose based on age, weight, medical condition, or other personal characteristics.

Do not start, stop, change, or adjust a medication or dosage based solely on information displayed by the App.

Always follow your prescription, medicine packaging, and the advice of a qualified doctor, pharmacist, or other healthcare professional.

If you have questions about a medicine, dosage, side effects, interactions, contraindications, or treatment, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

In case of a medical emergency, contact your local emergency medical service or seek immediate professional medical assistance.

3. Information You May Enter

Medicine Cabinet may allow you to voluntarily enter information necessary for the App's medication organization and reminder features. Depending on the features available in your version of the App, this may include:

3.1 Profile and Patient Information

  • Name or profile name.
  • Basic information voluntarily entered by the user.
  • Patient-related information voluntarily entered by the user.

3.2 Medication Information

  • Medicine name.
  • Generic or active ingredient name.
  • Medicine category.
  • User-entered prescribed dose or quantity.
  • Reminder schedule.
  • Frequency.
  • Start and end dates.
  • Notes or instructions entered by the user.

3.3 Medication Reminder History

  • Taken reminder records.
  • Skipped reminder records.
  • Snoozed reminder records.
  • Medication reminder schedules.
  • Medication history generated from reminder activity.

3.4 Doctor Appointment Information

  • Doctor name.
  • Appointment date and time.
  • Appointment notes voluntarily entered by the user.
  • Appointment reminder preferences.

4. How Information Is Used

Information entered into Medicine Cabinet may be used only to provide the functionality of the App, including:

  • Creating and managing medication reminders.
  • Delivering scheduled medication notifications.
  • Providing optional voice reminders using device text-to-speech capabilities.
  • Recording and displaying reminder activity.
  • Displaying medication history.
  • Organizing medicines and medication schedules.
  • Creating doctor appointment reminders.
  • Displaying general medicine reference information.

Medicine Cabinet does not use user-entered medication or patient information to provide a medical diagnosis or personalized treatment recommendation.

5. General Medicine Reference Information

The App may provide general educational and reference information about medicines or their active ingredients when requested by the user.

Depending on the available medicine reference data, this information may include a generic name, general purpose, medicine category, general warnings, or other non-personalized reference information.

Important: Medicine reference information is provided for general educational purposes only. It is not personalized medical advice and should not be used to determine or change a medicine or dosage.

Users should verify important medication information with a qualified healthcare professional, pharmacist, official medicine documentation, or another appropriate authoritative source.

6. Data Storage

Medicine Cabinet is designed to store medication, reminder, appointment, and profile information locally on the user's device.

Locally stored information is not intended to be transmitted to our servers or stored in a developer-operated cloud database.

The information stored locally remains under the control of the device and the user. If the App provides a future backup, synchronization, authentication, or cloud-storage feature, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when that functionality is introduced.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on the device until the user deletes it through available App functionality, clears the App's application data, or uninstalls the App.

Because locally stored records are not maintained on our servers, we generally cannot remotely access, retrieve, or delete those records on the user's behalf.

Users are responsible for maintaining any personal backup they may require.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Medication and patient information stored locally by the App is not intentionally shared with third parties through a developer-operated cloud database.

If the App uses a third-party service for a specific functionality, the applicable service and its data practices will be disclosed in this Privacy Policy where required.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

9. Notifications and Voice Reminders

Medicine Cabinet may schedule notifications to remind users about medicines and doctor appointments.

If voice reminders are enabled, the App may use the device's text-to-speech functionality to speak reminder information.

These reminder features are intended to provide organizational assistance and do not constitute medical advice.

Notification permissions can be managed through the Android device settings.

10. App Permissions

Medicine Cabinet requests only permissions required for its available functionality.

Depending on the Android version and App implementation, this may include:

  • Notifications: Used to deliver medication and appointment reminders.
  • Exact alarms or scheduling-related access: Used where Android requires such access for timely medication or appointment reminders.
  • Other permissions: Only permissions required by an implemented feature may be requested.

Permissions can be reviewed and managed through Android device settings.

Such information may include app version, operating system version, crash information, diagnostic information, or similar technical data, depending on the actual services integrated into the App.

We do not intentionally use technical diagnostic information to identify or profile users for medical purposes.

Users should protect their device with an appropriate screen lock, keep their operating system updated, and avoid sharing their device with unauthorized persons.
