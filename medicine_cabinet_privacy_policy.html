1. Introduction

Medicine Cabinet ("we", "us", "our", or the "App") is a medicine reminder and medication organization application designed to help users manage medicine schedules, dose reminders, patient profiles, medicine history, doctor appointments, and related personal organization features.

By using Medicine Cabinet, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of the App.

Medicine Cabinet is intended as a reminder and organization tool. It does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medicines, replace a healthcare professional, or provide emergency medical services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the App may handle the following types of information:

2.1 Patient and Profile Information

  • Name or profile name.
  • Basic profile details that you voluntarily enter.
  • Patient-related information you choose to store in the App.

2.2 Medicine Information

  • Medicine name and generic name.
  • Dosage or quantity.
  • Medicine category.
  • Schedule, reminder time, frequency, start date, and end date.
  • Notes or instructions you voluntarily enter.

2.3 Reminder and Medication History

  • Taken, skipped, or snoozed dose records.
  • Reminder schedules and notification preferences.
  • Medication history and monthly adherence/analysis information.

2.4 Doctor Appointment Information

  • Appointment date and time.
  • Doctor name or appointment notes, if entered by you.
  • Reminder preferences associated with an appointment.

2.5 Technical Information

The App may process limited technical information necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the service, depending on the configuration and services used by the App. This may include device or application information such as operating system version, app version, crash information, and diagnostic information.

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by Medicine Cabinet may be used to:

  • Create and manage medicine schedules and reminders.
  • Deliver scheduled medicine notifications and voice reminders.
  • Record and display medication history.
  • Generate monthly medication analysis and adherence information.
  • Organize medicines by category.
  • Manage patient profiles and doctor appointment reminders.
  • Provide medicine-related information when requested by the user.
  • Improve app reliability, performance, and security.
  • Respond to support requests and user communications.

We do not use your personal information to provide medical diagnosis or treatment recommendations.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant functionality, comply with applicable legal requirements, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain security.

If information is stored locally, uninstalling the App or clearing its application data may remove locally stored information. You should keep your own backup when the App provides an export or backup feature.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Notifications and Voice Reminders

Medicine Cabinet may schedule local notifications to remind you about medicines and doctor appointments. If you enable voice reminders, the App may use your device's text-to-speech capabilities to speak reminder information.

These features are intended to operate on your device and may require notification and related permissions. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

Medicine Cabinet requests permissions only when they are needed for a feature. Depending on the version of the App and the features enabled, permissions may include:

You can review or change available permissions through your Android device settings.

Medicine Cabinet is a medication reminder and organization tool. The information entered by users and any medicine information displayed by the App should not be treated as professional medical advice.

Do not change, stop, or start a medicine solely because of information displayed in the App. For medical emergencies, contact your local emergency service or seek immediate professional medical assistance.
